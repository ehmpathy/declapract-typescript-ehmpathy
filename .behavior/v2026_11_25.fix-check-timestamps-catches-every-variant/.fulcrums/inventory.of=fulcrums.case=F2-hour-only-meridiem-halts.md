# F2 — an hour-only meridiem halts

## .the fork
- A: halt `hpm`, `h am`, `hh a.m.` (no minutes)
- B: halt a meridiem only when minutes are present

## .taken, why
A. "at `h`pm" maps when a person is awake as surely as `h:mm`. the meridiem marker makes the form
unambiguous: a count of project managers (`n PMs`) is cut by the right edge, which forbids a letter after.

## .rework, why
clean: make the minutes group required in one form.

## .confidence, why low
85%. an hour-only time is coarser than a minute clock; one could argue it leaks less. the wish's
"every time-of-day variant" reads as one that holds it too.
