# 1.vision.experience.case=3 — a scale ratio halts

inventory row 17. feel: sharp. care: alterpath (rare in this corpus: zero hits in 2674 files).

## .narrative

a dev writes "a `1:nn` scale model" in a readme. the one-digit-hour form reads it as a clock and
halts the commit. the error names the line; the dev rewrites it as `1-to-nn` or `1/nn` and the
commit lands.

this is the price of row 10 (`h:mm` halts). a one-digit-hour clock in prose is a real leak; a scale
ratio with a two-digit right side shares its shape exactly. the hook cannot tell them apart, and its
law is to fail toward privacy. fulcrum F1 records the call.
