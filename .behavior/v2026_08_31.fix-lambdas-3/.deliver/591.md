## defect 1 — the two cicd param names are now SLASH paths, stageless (the in-repo fix)

`persist-with-rds/best-practice/provision/aws/resources.parameters.ts` declared the two cicd
credentials as DOTTED, stage-scoped names (`${org}.${project}.${stage}.database.role.cicd.for-plan.password`).
the prod plan role pins its ONE `kms:Decrypt` exception to a SLASH arn
(`parameter/*/svc-*/database/role/cicd/for-plan/*`) that needs literal `/` separators and omits a
stage (the aws ACCOUNT separates prep from prod, never the name). a dotted name cannot match that
arn, so a repo that ports the wish verbatim creates a param its plan role can never decrypt — and
does not find out until prod.

ported the shape from the shipped `ahbode/svc-home-services` (which agrees with the iam pin):
- **for-plan / for-apply** → `/${org}/${project}/database/role/cicd/for-{plan,apply}/password` —
  slash path, NO stage segment, byte-identical to the pin.
- **crud** → keeps its dotted `${namespace}.database.role.crud.password` (no policy pins it; matched
  by config/${env}.json).

added a `.note` on why the three names differ in shape by design, plus inline comments on each entry.
also aligned the practice readme: the decrypt-exception prose now names the slash path, and the
"prep→dev name cast" section now states only the crud name casts — the two cicd names carry no stage.
