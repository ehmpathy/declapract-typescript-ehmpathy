# 🌙 dream: rhx shows a skill's stderr on success, or says it hides it

- kind: reseed (owner: rhachet)
- caught: 2026-11-02

## .the cue that fired

a self review walked `rhx git.attr.get` by hand. the skill writes a treestruct banner to stderr so its stdout pipes clean. via `rhx`, the banner never appeared: `rhx` replays a skill's stderr only when the skill fails, and drops it on success.

## .the gap, precisely

- `rhx <skill>` on exit 0: the skill's stderr is discarded. on exit != 0: it is replayed under rhx's "blocked" header
- a skill author who sends status to stderr (the unix convention for status beside data) gets a silent skill when a human calls it through rhx
- stdout via rhx is not clean either: rhx prepends its own header and blank lines. so a skill gains no pipe-clean stdout from the split, and loses its status line

## .the shape of the fix

one of, in rhachet:
1. pass a skill's stderr through on success, as on failure
2. or keep the drop, and state it in the enroller brief on skill stdout (`howto.write.skills-stdout`), so authors put their banner on stdout

## .why it is not done in this round

the behavior lives in rhachet, another repo. this repo fixed its side: `git.attr.get`'s help now states that via rhx a success prints rhx's header, then git's lines.
