# case=2: reviewer reads a route pr

cells: rv2 · rv10 — happy · critipath

## .narrative

a clone drove a route to completion. the pr carries the route dir: stones, guards, a dozen `.reviews/`, `.route/` caches, `.fulcrums/`, `.seeds/`, three `*.yield.md` files, and a `.radio/` task record. plus the code.

before: the human scrolls through stone prompts and review transcripts they never wrote and will never act on.

after: the route dir shows exactly two kinds of file, open: the `*.yield.md` files (what the clone concluded) and the `.radio/` records (what the clone handed off). all else in the route is collapsed, one click away. the human reads the vision and verification yields, checks the radio handoff, then reviews the code.

the aha: the yields ARE the route's output. the rest is the trail, kept for audit, out of the way.
