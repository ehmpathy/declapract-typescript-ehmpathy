## #547's original claim

"the `.dpdmrc.yaml` template must MERGE a repo's own excludes, not clobber them with `exclude: []`."
the premise: `exclude: []` looked like a template that wipes a consumer's dpdm exclude list.

## why the resolution reversed it — #581

on closer read (settled by #581, delivered this wish), `exclude: []` is NOT a clobber — it is
**deliberate org policy**. dpdm's OWN default excludes `/node_modules/`; an empty `exclude: []`
renders `--exclude ""` (dpdm's include-all value), so dpdm checks ALL files for cycles, **dependencies
included**. that is exactly what we want: a prod-dep cycle cannot ship to a consumer unseen.

so a MERGE of a repo's excludes (what #547 asked for) would be the DEFECT — it would let a consumer
re-add `node_modules` and silently re-enable dpdm's default, which blinds the cycle check across every
repo. #547's concern (the dpdm exclude behavior is wrong) was real; its proposed fix pointed the
wrong way.

## what shipped instead

- `lint/best-practice/.dpdmrc.yaml` — keeps `exclude: []`, with a comment that states WHY it is empty.
- `rule.forbid.dpdm-exclude-change` (mechanic-role brief) — forbids a future tidy-up that adds
  `node_modules` / `^node_modules` or "corrects" the list toward dpdm's default.
- `lint/dpdmrc-exclude-policy.declapract.test.ts` (practice-root clamp, never emitted) — asserts the
  shipped `exclude` is an array, is empty, and holds no `node_modules` entry. GREEN this pass (3
  passed). teeth: add any `node_modules` entry to the template and the "no node_modules" assertion
  reddens.

## net

#547 is closed: the dpdm exclude behavior is now deliberate, guarded by a brief + a teeth'd clamp.
the fix is #581's (make the empty state deliberate), not #547's (merge) — because a merge is the
exact hazard the policy exists to forbid.
