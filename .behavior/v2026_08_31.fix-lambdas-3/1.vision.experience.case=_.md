## why these are the critipaths (care = freq × cost)

- **1, 2, 3** — high freq (every consumer / every cross-call / every service migration) × high cost
  (silent infra break / prep 404 / suite cannot pass). the happy path that must stay happy.
- **4, 5** — low freq (a rare author moment) × catastrophic cost (silent IAM break / terminal
  unrepairable repo). the sharp author paths that must fail safe.
- **6** — high freq (a check that fires on every consumer's every run) × invisible-drift cost. a
  critipath for the *frequency* half, not rarity: the silent check runs constantly, so a check that
  cannot fire hides drift on every run. the sharp path that must speak, not stay silent.
- **7** — low freq (once per service, per env) × catastrophic cost (a silent stateful split-brain).
  the sharp operator path made unreachable by contract.
- **8** — low freq (a dep bumps a syntax floor / a `.nvmrc` moves) × catastrophic cost (a lambda that
  cannot parse, so prod alone breaks while every gate is green). the sharp path made loud: a gate
  runs the artifact on the node that will serve it, so the break fails a check, not prod.
