# 🌙 dream: the director's wish writer keeps the deadline clock out of 0.wish.md

kind: reseed · owner: the director's dispatch (nheuron direction) · caught 2026-11-15

## .the cue that fired
the first commit of this route was refused by `check.timestamps.sh`: `0.wish.md` carried
`hour: green by <date> <clock>Z`. the hook takes zero exemptions, so the driver masked the clock
to `[time]` in the tracked wish, which drops the deadline from the record.

## .the gap, precisely
the writer that authors `0.wish.md` stamps an absolute clock into a file the route commits. every
route it seeds hits the same refusal, and every driver must edit the wisher's words to commit.

## .the shape of the fix
keep the deadline out of the tracked wish: carry it in the dispatch message, or in an untracked
route file (e.g. `.route/deadline` under the route's `.gitignore`), or as a duration
(`hour: green within PT2H`). the wish states the outcome; the clock rides beside it.

## .why it is not done in this round
it lives in the director's dispatch, not in this repo.
