# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space of "main requires one review approval", once the provision-github practice
declares it and an adopter applies it.

## .dimensions

| # | dimension | values |
|---|-----------|--------|
| D1 | moment | `plan` (provision plan after declapract apply) · `merge` (a pr into main) · `census` (read the fleet) |
| D2 | pr author | `bot` (seaturtle[bot]) · `human` · `release` (rhelease app's release pr) |
| D3 | review state at merge | `none` · `approved` · `approved-then-pushed` |
| D4 | repo state before apply | `lacks` (protected, no review rule) · `unprotected` (no protection) · `requires` (already gated) · `unadopted` (provision-github not in its use.yml) |
| D5 | humans with write | `many` (an org with a second reviewer) · `one` (a solo owner) |
| D6 | viewpoint at merge | `author` (bot or mechanic, via `git.release --watch`) · `reviewer` (the human who must approve) |

D1 picks which other dimensions apply: `plan` varies on D4; `merge` varies on D2 × D3 × D5;
`census` varies on D4 alone.

## .the walked product

- `plan` × D4 → 4 cells
- `merge` × D2 × D3 × D5 → 3 × 3 × 2 = 18 cells, collapsed where D5 cannot matter
  (a `bot` or `release` pr has a non-author human to approve whenever any human has write)
- `merge` × D6 at `review = none` → 2 cells: the author's watch, and the reviewer's notice
  (D6 only splits where a review is owed; once approved, both views agree)
- `census` × D4 → 1 cell: one read covers all four D4 values, mapped to verdicts in the inventory
  (`unadopted` reads as `lacks`, since the census reads github, not use.yml). its third verdict,
  `unknown`, is a read gap, not a D4 value
- `merge` × `human` × D3 × D5 → walked in full: 3 × 2 = 6 cells, two of them impossible
  (a solo owner cannot obtain an approval)

every cell is verdicted in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.
