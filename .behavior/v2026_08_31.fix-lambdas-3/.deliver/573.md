## the defect — a practice stamps a caller without its callee, at three grains

`cicd-service` stamps callers (`provision.yml`, `deploy.yml`) whose callees declare files, secrets,
or conversions the caller never supplies — a repo that is quietly wrong, unreported by any gate.

## D28 — provision.yml's 4 rds jobs, guarded behind persist-with-rds (loud) — LANDED

`provision.yml` carried 4 rds jobs (`sql-schema-prep/prod`, `aws-prep/prod-declastruct`) that point
at files `persist-with-rds` owns, so a repo on a non-rds service usecase red-failed its own
`provision` workflow. the 5.1 execution landed a `provision.yml.declapract.ts` `contents` fn that
emits the full superset when `context.projectPractices.includes('persist-with-rds')` and STRIPS the 4
rds jobs when it does not — the generic jobs (`github`, `aws-*-terraform`) survive either way.

clamped two ways:
- **unit** (`provision.yml.declapract.test.ts`, 5 tests) — GREEN this pass.
- **end-to-end pipeline** (`provision.yml.declapract.integration.test.ts`, 10 tests) — GREEN this
  pass. runs the REAL `executeApply` against a `genTempDir` consumer for BOTH usecases: rds keeps all
  4 jobs, non-rds strips all 4, generics survive both, twice-applied is a fixed point, full emitted
  snapshots for a reviewer to eyeball. scoped by `file` (never `--practice`, which would strip
  `persist-with-rds` from `projectPractices` and silence the branch) against a lean `declarations/`
  mirror, per `rule.require.declapract-integration-tests`.

## D29 — deploy.yml passes the pagerduty secret its callee declares (silent) — LANDED

`.deploy-sls.yml` declares `pagerduty-integration-key` `required: false` and gates its `alarm on
failure` step on `if: failure() && env.PAGERDUTY_INTEGRATION_KEY` — so a caller that omits the secret
leaves the alarm step SKIPPED (reads as success) and every prod alert dead. worse, `deploy.yml` is
EQUALS, so a `fix` STRIPPED a consumer's own pass-through — alerts died by conformance. the 5.1
execution landed the `prod` job's `secrets:` block that hands the key through.

clamped by `cicd-service/.declapract.integration.test.ts` (7 tests, GREEN this pass): the callee
DECLARES the secret under `workflow_call.secrets` + USES it in a step, and the caller's prod job
hands it through to a REAL `.deploy-sls.yml` call (guarded from vacuity). this IS the focused
caller↔callee reconciliation guard #573 asks for. a bonus `[t2]` clamp catches the prod test-gate
precedence bug (github-actions `&&` binds tighter than `||`, so a `v*` tag push could deploy on a
failed test) — the silent-break class the wish fights.
