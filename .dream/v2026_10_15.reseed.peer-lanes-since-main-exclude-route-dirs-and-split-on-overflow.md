# 🌙 dream: a since-main peer lane reviews its own route dir, and an overflowed lane fails rather than split

- kind = reseed
- owner = ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain (`review`), with ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild (the
  behavior route's guard template)
- caught 2026-10-15

## .the cue that fired

on `declapract-typescript-ehmpathy` @ `beav/fix-absorb-recent-upgrade-reports`, stone
`5.1.execution.from_vision`, peer round i001: all 9 l1 lanes refused at 135.2% of the window
(771 files, 1279.2k tokens) before they read a line. the route was wide: 108 fixes and their tests.

## .the gap, precisely

1. **the route reviews itself.** each l1 lane binds `--diffs since-main` with no `--paths-wout`,
   so the route's own artifacts enter the review: `.behavior/` alone was 312.6k tokens (24%), and
   `.dream/` another 16.4k. those are route archaeology, not the change under review.
2. **an overflow is a dead end.** `review` fails fast past 75% with "reduce scope or use --focus
   pull". it names no split, and the guard template gives the driver no way to make one: the
   route is sealed, so a guard edit needs a human grant. a wide route therefore halts on a wall
   only a human can move, and every wide route meets it.

## .the shape of the fix

- **guard template (bhuild):** every since-main peer lane carries `--paths-wout '.behavior/**'
  --paths-wout '.dream/**'` (and `.route/**`) by default.
- **review (bhrain):** on overflow, measure the scope and emit a path split that fits: bucket the
  target files by top dirs until each bucket sits under ~60%. print the split as the exact peer
  commands to paste. better still, offer a mode where the lane fans out over the split on its own
  and tallies one verdict, so the guard's budget and judge lines need not change.
- clamp: a fixture route whose since-main scope is ~130% of the window. assert the default lane
  excludes the route dirs, and that an overflow prints a split whose every slice fits.

## .why it is not done in this round

both halves live in other repos (S5: no work in other repos). here the foreman's call is a
one-time guard edit, by grant: exclude the route dirs and split each l1 lane into 3 path slices.
