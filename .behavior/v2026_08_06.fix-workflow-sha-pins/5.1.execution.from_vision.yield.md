### two placement constraints, both measured

1. **the filename must end `.declapract.test.ts`** — `jest.unit.config.ts` has exactly that
   `testMatch`, and the config is itself a template (do not edit it to fit a test).
2. **the file may NOT sit in `src/practices/`** — `readPracticeDeclarations.js` calls
   `readdir` on that dir and treats **every entry** as a practice directory, files included.
   a stray `.ts` there would crash the declaration read. so it lives at `src/`.

### why the blind-spot guard (`[t0]`) exists, measured rather than supposed

8 of the 17 workflow templates are **dot-prefixed** (`.test.yml`, `.install.yml`,
`.declastruct.yml`, `.deploy-sls.yml`, `.terraform.yml`, `.sql-schema-control.yml`,
`.publish-npm.yml`, `.deploy-expo.yml`) and they hold most of the refs. a glob that omits
dotfiles by default walks past them in silence.

the injection proves the stakes: with a dot-blind walk, **all four content assertions stayed
green**. only `[t0]` went red. the walk uses node's own `readdir` for that reason, and `[t0]`
clamps the walk itself.

⚠️ this repo's own `rhx globsafe` has that blind spot — it reported 11 files where `grep`
found 17. the discrepancy is what surfaced the hazard.

### the `*.yml` bound carries load

`src/practices/cicd-common/best-practice/.github/workflows/__snapshots__/.test.yml.declapract.test.ts.snap`
holds `actions/checkout@v4` at lines 49 and 82 — a **fixture**, not a template. the walk
excludes `__snapshots__` outright and bounds to yaml; `[t0]` asserts both.

**what my own instruments got wrong, twice — and how each was caught**

this is the part worth the record, because in both cases the instrument reported success while
the defect was live.

1. **`^\s*uses:` missed the `- uses:` list-item form.** i nearly wrote a false "84" correction
   off it. caught by a direct read of `release.yml`, where the list-item form appears. the
   clamp's own parse now handles both shapes, and the `.note` on `getAllActionRefsIn` says why.
2. **`uses:\s*[^.\s]+@(?![0-9a-f]{40})` reported "No matches" while
   `hashicorp/setup-terraform@v3` was still unpinned** at `.test.yml:86`. caught only by a
   count of total (47) against pinned (46) — the difference of one, not the search.
3. **`rhx globsafe` reported 11 workflow files where `grep` found 17.** the missing 6 were all
   dot-prefixed. that discrepancy is what produced the `[t0]` blind-spot guard, and the
   injection later proved the stakes: with a dot-blind walk, every content assertion stayed
   green.

the through-line: **a negative result from a search is not evidence of absence.** all three
were caught by a *count* or a *direct read*, never by a refined pattern. the clamp exists so
that the next author does not have to re-derive that discipline under pressure.
